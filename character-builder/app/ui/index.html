      Shown the way the Tekken 3 select screen draws it: 168 × 252, in four bands of 64 colors.

      Letters from Tekken 3's name font (there is no F or Q), space, period, hyphen or 2. Up to 15 characters, and short enough for the name plate.

      Fighting style

      A style gives your fighter a complete moveset: the moves, animations and CPU behavior of the fighter it is based on.

      Credits and licences (your own assets)

      Where your 3D model, portrait image and recording come from. Packages carry this; Export warns when an asset in the package has no known licence.

      Body parts

      Mix body parts of Tekken 3's own fighters on your style's skeleton. Only fighters of a similar build are offered (height within 7 %). Check the result in the 3D preview; Undo is on the Model Importer page. The parts are read from your own Tekken 3 disc; packages carry only your choices.

      Costumes and colours

      Recolour the donor's costumes in the 3D preview. Each colour variant can replace a costume or become an extra costume in the game; variants are saved with your fighter.

      Own 3D model

      Give your fighter its own PS1-style 3D model. It must be rigged: a skeleton with skin weights (for example from Mixamo: upload, auto-rig, then download as FBX Binary in T-pose). The creator fits it to the fighting style's skeleton and replaces the style's model on the costume you choose.

      What makes a model work well?
      • Rigged human shape: a skeleton with a head, two arms and two legs, and every vertex weighted to a bone. Mixamo, Character Creator, Ready Player Me, VRoid and most game rigs work as they are; unusual bone names are recognised from the skeleton's shape.
      • Standing in T-pose or A-pose, arms away from the body.
      • File: .fbx (FBX Binary), .glb or .vrm (VRoid), up to 16 MB, with the texture embedded. Models with only material colours work too.
      • Any size: Tekken 3 fighters have 650-1100 triangles. A bigger model is reduced to that size and gets a new texture made for it (1 to 3 minutes).
      • Hair, tails and horns can't move on their own in Tekken 3: parts that stick out are folded down for you, and you can change that per part.
      • Clothes become part of the body shape. Long coats, capes and wide skirts move with the legs, so they look best when they are short or tight.

            Parts that stick out

            Tekken 3 can't swing hair, tails or horns: they stay where they are modelled. They are folded down for you; change it per part if you like.

              Detail settings apply to big models, which are reduced to PS1 size. Less face detail leaves more for the body; less body detail gives a simpler, more PS1-like model.

              Portrait

              With Tekken 3 Expanded your portrait appears on the select screen. With Tekken3Recompiled it is saved for a later version.

              Saved portraits

                Keep a portrait, try another, and click a kept one to use it again. The portrait in use is kept automatically when you switch.
